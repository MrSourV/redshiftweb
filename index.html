Breakout

Breakout evolved from the recently rebranded Prisoner Life, a classic Guards vs. Prisoners experience set within a prison environment and built around a modernized interpretation of old-school Roblox gameplay and aesthetics. As RedShift's first fully in-house title, Breakout marked the collective's debut as both a developer and publisher on the Roblox platform. Its success became a defining milestone for the collective, earning RedShift a place within Roblox's exclusive top developer program.
